# OpenBlast v1.0.7 Release Notes

**Version**: 1.0.7 (Build 49)  
**Developer**: Created by Masthan Valli  
**Release Date**: November 8, 2026  

## Highlights

### 1. Developer Attribution Across Every Platform
- **Android**: Featured under **Settings › About › Developer** as *"Created by Masthan Valli"*.
- **Desktop**: Featured prominently under **Settings › About** on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- **Official Website**: Featured on the official OpenBlast portal and footer.

### 2. Expanded Typography (3 New Typefaces)
Added 3 distinct, high-readability typefaces under **Settings › Appearance › Fonts & readability** without altering existing selections:
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: A clean, contemporary geometric grotesque designed for modern interfaces.
- **Lexend**: Engineered specifically to reduce visual stress and improve reading speed.
- **Inter**: World-renowned precision interface typeface with ultra-sharp definition.

### 3. Two New Accent Color Themes
Added 2 new vibrant color accents under **Settings › Appearance › Accent color**:
- **Electric Violet** (`#7C4DFF`): Deep energetic neon violet.
- **Sunset Coral** (`#FF5722`): Warm radiant dusk coral.
*All 10 existing accent colors remain completely preserved.*

### 4. High-Definition Launcher Mipmap Assets
- Generated complete raster mipmap suites (`mdpi`, `hdpi`, `xhdpi`, `xxhdpi`, `xxxhdpi`) for both standard and circular Android launcher specifications.
- Refreshed Android TV Leanback banner with crisp vector typography and brand emblem.
- Ensured 100% brand consistency across custom launchers (Vivo FunTouch OS / OriginOS, Samsung One UI, Xiaomi HyperOS, Pixel Launcher).
