# OpenBlast v2.0.2.1 Release Notes

**Version**: 2.0.2.1 (Build 55)  
**Developer**: Masthan Valli  
**Release Date**: November 12, 2026  
**Target Platform**: Android (Android 8.0+)

---

## What's New in v2.0.2.1

### 1. 📌 Persistent Music Notification
- **Always Accessible Controls**: Your media player notification now stays pinned in the notification shade and lock screen even when you pause your music or clear the app from recent tasks.
- **Instant Resume**: Tap play anytime directly from the notification to pick up right where you left off without delays or having to reopen the app.

### 2. 📱 Built-in QR Scanner for Listen Together
- **Scan Screen to Join**: Added a convenient camera scanner right inside the app. When joining a friend's room, simply tap "Scan QR" and point your camera at their screen to join immediately.
- **Native App Experience**: Room invites and QR codes now connect directly through the mobile app without bouncing through web browsers.

### 3. 👥 Jam & Blends Friend Recommendations
- **Recommend Songs for Friends**: Inside your Listen Together room, you can now suggest songs tailored for specific friends or the whole group.
- **In-Room Song Search**: Search for any track or pick from your liked songs without leaving the room.
- **Attribution Badges**: Songs queued by friends now display badges showing who recommended each song, bringing a fun collaborative vibe to your listening sessions.

### 4. ⏰ "Remind in 3 Days" Update Snooze
- **Listen Uninterrupted**: When an update notification appears, you can now tap "Remind in 3 days" to snooze reminders for 72 hours so it won't interrupt you every time you open the app.

### 5. 🔊 Enhanced Volume Boost
- **Louder, Cleaner Sound**: Enjoy enhanced volume amplification up to 200% with built-in soft-limiting that keeps music loud, full, and clear without distortion.

### 6. ✨ Interactive Lyrics Animation Preview
- **Live Preview in Settings**: When choosing your favorite lyrics motion effect in Settings, a live interactive card now shows you an animated preview in real time before you apply it.

### 7. 🎨 Polished Interface & Modern Icons
- **Clean Vector Badges**: Replaced raw text emojis with sleek modern icons across the player, queue, and Listen Together room.
