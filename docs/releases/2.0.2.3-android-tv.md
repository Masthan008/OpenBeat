**Version**: 2.0.2.3 (Build 57)

**Release Date**: November 17, 2026  
**Target Platform**: Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV, Smart TVs (Android 8.0+)

## What's New in v2.0.2.3 (Android TV Edition)

### 1. Dedicated 10-Foot Television Experience
- **Leanback Launcher Integration**: Features the official Android TV banner (`@drawable/tv_banner`) and leanback launcher category, placing OpenBlast directly on your smart TV's home screen.
- **Directional D-Pad Focus Traversal**: Seamless focus rings designed for high visibility from across the living room, ensuring smooth navigation with any television remote.
- **TV Remote Multimedia Keys**: Native hardware media key support (`Play`, `Pause`, `Fast-Forward`, `Rewind`, `Next`, and `Previous`).

### 2. Listen Together (Jam Sessions) on the Big Screen
- **Sync & Unsync Controls**: Roommates and guests can now enjoy listening in synchronization with the room or switch to independent listening directly on Android TV.
- **Collaborative Playback**: Smooth, drift-free room playback when the host grants control permissions to other members.

### 3. Clean Home Feed & Performance
- **Distraction-Free Living Room UI**: Completely removed community prompts and legacy Discord notices across Android TV screens.
- **TV Hardware Optimization**: Hardware telephony, camera, and touchscreen dependencies are disabled, ensuring lightweight, high-performance streaming across all smart TVs, streaming sticks, and projectors.

## How to Install on Android TV

### Method 1: Downloader App (Fastest & Simplest)
1. Install and open the **Downloader** app from the Google Play Store on your TV.
2. Enter the direct link or browse to:
   `https://github.com/Masthan008/OpenBeat/releases/download/v2.0.2.3-android-tv/OpenBlast-v2.0.2.3-android-tv-universal.apk`
3. Click **Install**. OpenBlast will appear on your TV home screen!

### Method 2: Sideload via USB or "Send Files to TV"
1. Download `OpenBlast-v2.0.2.3-android-tv-universal.apk` on your phone or PC.
2. Transfer it to your TV using a USB flash drive or the **Send Files to TV** app.
3. Open any file manager on your TV (such as File Commander) and click the APK to install.
