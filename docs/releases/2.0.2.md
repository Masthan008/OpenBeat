# OpenBlast v2.0.2 Release Notes

**Version**: 2.0.2 (Build 54)  
**Developer**: Created by Masthan Valli  
**Release Date**: November 12, 2026  
**Target Platform**: Android (Android 7.0+)

---

## What's New in v2.0.2

### 1. 💾 Persistent Playlist Backup (Survives Uninstalls)
- **Survives App Uninstall**: Backups are written directly to public external storage at `Documents/OpenBlast/backups/openblast_playlists_backup.json` (with a mirror in `Downloads/OpenBlast/backups/`). Even if you uninstall OpenBlast, your playlists and starred tracks are safely retained on your phone!
- **Automatic Re-Install Restoration**: When you reinstall OpenBlast fresh, it automatically detects your existing persistent backup on first launch and restores your entire library seamlessly without any extra clicks.
- **Manual Backup & Restore**: New buttons under **Settings → Your data** let you create a snapshot backup or restore from backup anytime.

### 2. ⏯️ Notification Bar Playback Resumption Fix
- **Instant Playback Resumption**: Resolved the idle state deadlock when the app was killed or cleared from recents. Tapping **Play** directly on the pinned media notification in the notification bar now prepares the player and starts playback immediately—no need to open the app first!

### 3. 🔊 VLC-Style 200% Volume Boost
- **Supercharged Audio**: Boost sound up to **200% (+6.0 dB)** beyond standard 100% Android volume for quiet tracks, headphones, or noisy environments.
- **Soft-Limiting Protection**: Integrated smooth hyperbolic tangent (`tanh`) soft-limiting in the audio DSP engine to prevent harsh digital clipping or distortion at high volumes.
- **Accessible Everywhere**: Adjust boost from the **Remix sheet** in the Now Playing player or from **Settings → Equalizer**.

### 4. 👥 Listen Together Jam & Instagram Blends Mode
- **Blend Attribution Badges**: Songs in shared Listen Together Jam sessions now carry Instagram Blends-style tags in the queue and player (`✨ Suggested for [Person]` and `🎧 Picked by [Person]`).
- Share and blend music effortlessly with friends in real-time rooms.
