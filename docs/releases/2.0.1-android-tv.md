# OpenBlast v2.0.1 (Android TV Edition) Release Notes

**Version**: 2.0.1 (Build 53)  
**Developer**: Created by Masthan Valli  
**Release Date**: November 11, 2026  
**Target Platform**: Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV, Smart TVs (Android 7.0+)

---

## Android TV Release Highlights

### 1. Dedicated 10-Foot Leanback Interface
- **Widescreen Landscape TV Layout**: Specifically tailored for television screens and monitors with wide 220dp album artwork, landscape navigation rail, and ergonomic viewing distances.
- **TV Home Screen Launcher Banner**: Includes the official `@drawable/tv_banner` (320x180) and `<category android:name="android.intent.category.LEANBACK_LAUNCHER" />`, placing OpenBlast directly on your Android TV home screen row alongside YouTube and Netflix.

### 2. Full Remote Control & D-Pad Traversal
- **D-Pad Navigation**: Seamless directional focus traversal across all screens, playlists, search results, and settings using standard TV remote arrow keys.
- **Focused Visual Ring**: High-contrast, glowing focus rings make it clear which element is currently selected from across the room.
- **TV Remote Media Keys**: Native support for TV remote multimedia buttons (`Play`, `Pause`, `Play/Pause Toggle`, `Next`, `Previous`, `Fast-Forward`, `Rewind`, and `Stop`).
- **Remote Queue Management**: Dedicated "Move Up" and "Move Down" buttons on the TV player pane for effortless queue reordering without drag-and-drop.

### 3. TV Hardware Performance & Battery Optimizations
- **Hardware-Agnostic Operation**: Touchscreen, camera, telephony, and microphone hardware requirements are flagged as optional (`required="false"`), guaranteeing compatibility with all Android TV boxes, streaming sticks, and projectors.
- **Smooth 60fps Television Rendering**: Optimized Compose rendering pipeline reduces shader overhead for smooth transitions even on budget streaming sticks.

## How to Install on Android TV

### Method 1: Downloader App (Fastest & Simplest)
1. Install and open the **Downloader** app from the Google Play Store on your TV.
2. Enter the download URL for the universal APK or enter:
   `https://github.com/Masthan008/OpenBeat/releases/download/v2.0.1-android-tv/OpenBlast-v2.0.1-android-tv-universal.apk`
3. Click **Install**. OpenBlast will appear on your TV home screen!

### Method 2: Sideload via USB or "Send Files to TV"
1. Download `OpenBlast-v2.0.1-android-tv-universal.apk` on your phone or PC.
2. Transfer it to your TV using a USB flash drive or the **Send Files to TV** app.
3. Open any file manager on your TV (such as File Commander) and click the APK to install.

### Method 3: ADB Sideload (For Developers & Power Users)
```bash
adb connect <YOUR_TV_IP_ADDRESS>:5555
adb install -r OpenBlast-v2.0.1-android-tv-universal.apk
```
