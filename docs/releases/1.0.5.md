# OpenBlast 1.0.5 Release Notes

OpenBlast 1.0.5 introduces a brand-new 4-widget pack, complete notification icon and About/Updates logo refresh, full application brand cleanup, floating synced lyrics bubble, and audio engine enhancements.

### ✨ Highlights & New Features

- **Expanded Home-Screen Widgets Pack**:
  - **Compact Player Pill (2x1 / 3x1)**: Sleek single-row floating pill with album thumbnail, marquee title, and playback controls.
  - **Retro Vinyl Turntable (2x2 / 3x3)**: Vintage vinyl record disc with circular rotating cover art, grooved texture, and center spindle play/pause.
  - **Live Synced Lyrics Widget (4x2 / 4x3)**: Real-time synchronized lyrics and playback dock right on your home screen.
  - **Speed Dial & Quick Favorites (4x2)**: Instant-tap launcher tiles for *Liked Songs*, *Recents*, *Offline Downloads*, and *Supermix* with a mini-player bar.

- **Notification & Visual Polish**:
  - Replaced legacy status bar and notification shade vectors with the crisp white OpenBlast mark.
  - Updated the Settings → About → Updates dialog hero logo and canvas visuals.
  - Swept and updated all remaining user-facing "OpenTune" references across Settings, Support, Sharing Cards, Modals, and Car Mode.

- **Multitasking & Floating Lyrics Bubble**:
  - Floating lyrics overlay (`FloatingLyricsService`) displays synchronized lyrics and play/pause controls over other apps and games.

- **Audio Engine Upgrades**:
  - Proactive 3-track queue lookahead pre-caching (`WARM_AHEAD = 3`) for instantaneous, zero-gap track transitions.
  - Mono audio channel mixing in Audio DSP for single-earbud listening.
  - Decoupled update checking and release channels directly to `Masthan008/OpenBeat`.

### 📦 Installation
Install over existing versions to preserve your library and preferences. Choose **arm64-v8a** for most modern phones or **universal** if unsure.
