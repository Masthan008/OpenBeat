# OpenBlast v1.0.7 (iOS Edition)

OpenBlast for iPhone and iPad brings high-fidelity streaming, synced lyrics, and ad-free listening to iOS via modern sideloading channels.

### What's New in v1.0.7 (iOS)
- **Direct Progressive Audio Playback Fix**: Prioritized direct, unciphered progressive AAC (`audio/mp4`, 128 kbps) stream extraction, resolving playback stalls and audio loading failures caused by YouTube HLS multi-variant manifests.
- **Instant Stream Buffering**: Improved audio playback responsiveness and buffer startup times on iOS 16, 17, and 18.
- **SideStore & AltStore Sideload Alignment**: Fully compatible with official OpenBlast SideStore and AltStore sources under Masthan Valli.
- **Lock Screen & Now Playing**: Smooth Now Playing metadata synchronization and remote control response.
- **AirPlay 2 Streaming**: High-quality lossless wireless audio casting to HomePods, Apple TV, and compatible sound systems.
- **OpenBlast Sideload Source**: Directly install and receive updates in SideStore and AltStore by adding:
  `https://openbeat-site.vercel.app/ios/source.json`

### Installation Guide for iPhone & iPad
1. **SideStore / AltStore**:
   - Open SideStore or AltStore on your iPhone or iPad.
   - Go to **Sources** → **+** and paste: `https://openbeat-site.vercel.app/ios/source.json`
   - Tap **Install** or **Update** next to OpenBlast.
2. **Direct IPA Sideload**:
   - Download `OpenBlast-v1.0.7-ios.ipa` attached below.
   - Install using SideStore, AltStore, Sideloadly, TrollStore, or Xcode on iOS 16.0 or newer.

### Build Verification
- **Package**: `OpenBlast-v1.0.7-ios.ipa`
- **Target OS**: iOS 16.0+
