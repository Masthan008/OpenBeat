# Privacy Policy for OpenBlast

**Last updated:** November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **OpenBlast** mobile application ("the App"). OpenBlast is a free and open-source music player designed to respect user privacy.

---

### 1. Summary
* **No Personal Data Collection:** OpenBlast does not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal details (such as your name, phone number, physical address, or contacts).
* **No In-App Ads or Trackers:** OpenBlast is completely free of advertisements, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising SDKs.
* **Local-First Storage:** Your listening history, saved playlists, downloads, and playback settings are stored locally on your device.

---

### 2. Information Handled by the App

#### A. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
* **Music & Playlists:** Your favorites, custom playlists, and downloaded tracks are stored solely on your device.
* **App Preferences:** Settings such as equalizer configurations, theme choices, and lyrics animation preferences are saved locally.
* **Cache:** Album artwork and lyrics are temporarily cached on your device to ensure fast, offline playback.
* You can erase all local data at any time by clearing the app's data in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

#### B. Network Requests to Third-Party Services
To stream audio, display synced lyrics, and play radio stations, OpenBlast sends standard HTTP requests to public service providers:
* **Audio & Catalogue:** Public music stream endpoints to retrieve the music you choose to play.
* **Lyrics Services:** Services like LRCLIB to retrieve timed, synchronized lyrics.
* **Radio & Podcasts:** Public directory servers (such as Radio Browser) to list and play live radio streams.

These network requests include only the parameters required to deliver the content (such as search queries or track IDs) and your standard network connection information (IP address). OpenBlast does not sell or share this data.

---

### 3. Permissions Requested and Why

OpenBlast requests only the minimal Android permissions necessary to function:
* **Notifications / Post Notifications:** To display music playback controls and album art in the status bar and lock screen.
* **Foreground Service (Media Playback):** To ensure continuous, uninterrupted music playback when the screen is locked or while using other apps.
* **Audio / Media Files (Optional):** To scan and play local songs stored on your device storage, only if you choose to play local files.
* **Microphone (Optional):** Used strictly in real-time if you explicitly use the song recognition feature. No recordings are stored or uploaded without your explicit action.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
OpenBlast does not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is rated for all ages.

---

### 5. Security of Your Information
Because OpenBlast does not maintain user databases or cloud accounts, your listening data remains under your physical control on your device.
